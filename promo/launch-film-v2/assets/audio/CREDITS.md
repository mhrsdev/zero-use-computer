# Audio credits

## Music

**"Vibe Ace"** by Kevin MacLeod ([incompetech.com](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/music.html))
Licensed under [Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- File: `music/vibe-ace.ogg`, unchanged. It was taken from the
  [librosa example-data repository](https://github.com/librosa/data)
  (`audio/Kevin_MacLeod_-_Vibe_Ace.hq.ogg`), whose metadata gives the
  license as CC-BY-4.0 and the source as
  <https://freemusicarchive.org/music/Kevin_MacLeod/Jazz_Sampler/Vibe_Ace>.
- Changes made for the film (`audio/score.py`): cut and rearranged on the
  track's own beats, a low-pass filter sweep over the first 3.5 s, two
  tape-stop effects, and sound effects mixed over it. The track is not
  time-stretched or re-pitched.
- The credit is shown on the film's end card and stored in the MP4's
  metadata.
